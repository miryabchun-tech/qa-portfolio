# BUG-001 — Incorrect product images on Products page

## Environment

- Application: SauceDemo
- User: `problem_user`
- Browser: Google Chrome
- Page: Products

## Preconditions

User is logged in as `problem_user`.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open SauceDemo.
2. Login with `problem_user`.
3. Enter password `secret_sauce`.
4. Open the Products page.
5. Observe the images displayed on the product cards.
6. Open one of the products.
7. Observe the product image on the Product Details page.

## Expected Result

Each product card on the Products page should display the image corresponding to that product.

## Actual Result

All product cards on the Products page display the same image of a dog.

When a product is opened, the Product Details page displays the correct image for that product.

## Severity

Medium

## Priority

Medium

## Status

Open
